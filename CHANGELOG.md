### Added

- `tools/corpus-thumbs --preview <max> <quality>`: one WebP per image instead of a sheet, for full-screen viewing
- `scripts/build_removal_review.py` — the removal review page: the contact sheet with click-to-mark removals saved in the page's database, retired duplicates dropped, 1214 as its camera original, DNGs by their embedded previews, and the 28 empty or broken entries flagged
- `benchmarks/signal_index_2026-09-24.md`: 18 iPhone 16/17 Pro HEICs (depth map referencing both the primary and the ISO 21496-1 `tmap`) fail to open in libheif 1.17.6, Ubuntu 24.04's version, and in vips and ImageMagick built on it; libheif 1.21.2 and zen's heic decode them; the camera export wrote them this way
- `benchmarks/apple_legacy_gainmap_iso_fit_2026-10-03.md` and `scripts/apple_legacy_iso_fit.py`: ISO 21496-1 parameters can only approximate Apple's legacy gain curve over the same gain-map pixels (worst case 0.023–0.076 stops across the 24 legacy HEICs' headrooms); re-encoding the gain maps in ISO form stays within 0.006 stops
- `benchmarks/signal_index_2026-09-24.md`: the 3 DNGs fail to decode because they store lossless JPEG with restart intervals, which neither zenraw backend handles (rawler 0.8.0, rawloader 0.37.2; root cause corrected on zenraw#17); LibRaw and rawspeed decode all three, and 1458's LibRaw "Data corrupted" warning predates the corpus while the file itself is sound
- `tools/metadata-loss-audit/oracle_check.py`: checks whitelist-v2 outputs against their sources with exiftool, libjpeg-turbo and libheif (tag values, validation warnings, pixels incl. auxiliary images, gain-map and motion-photo extraction); it caught v2 dropping the JPEG-required `ExifImageWidth`/`Height`, which v2 now keeps
- `scripts/whitelist_v2.py` keeps the subject location: EXIF `SubjectArea` (57 Apple HEICs) and the camera's focus regions, regenerated as the only content of the HEIC main-image XMP (30 files), for region-weighted quality work
- `tools/resave-colour-check` — locates a re-saved crop in its camera original and tests which colour handling the re-save applied; on 1214 the tool that made the crop treated the P3 original as sRGB and converted it to Display P3 without a profile
- `tools/corpus-signal-probe`: `exif_colorspace` and `exif_interop` columns; the signal index now counts EXIF `ColorSpace` = 1 and Interop `R98` as sRGB declarations, so 38 camera JPEGs are no longer listed as untagged. In the camera folders only 1214 (a re-saved crop) and the 3 GoPro files (whose `R98` the metadata rewrite removed) declare nothing
- `tools/corpus-signal-probe`: `gain_map_params` column with every gain-map parameter (zenjpeg UltraHDR reader for JPEG, heic `probe_full` for HEIC); confirms whitelist v2 keeps all 76 HDR files' parameters identical to the camera originals
- `scripts/whitelist_v2.py` — re-applies the camera-class metadata whitelist to the oldest copies with container-aware code: same privacy policy, but every colour/HDR structure kept (per-item HEIC `colr`, UltraHDR MPF + gain map + ISO 21496-1, Apple MakerNote reduced to its three HDR tags, Interop `R98`, motion-photo XMP); HEIC rewritten in place (nothing moves), JPEG segments removed with MPF rebuilt; all 316 outputs match the oldest sources on every probed colour/HDR signal, decode, and carry no removed value; outputs on block storage, swapping them in is an owner decision
- `benchmarks/metadata_audit_earlier_copies_2026-09-25.md` — every source compared with earlier copies of the same image data: the 1,792 untagged JPEGs/PNGs lost no colour tag (none of their earlier copies had one); the HDR loss is the 33 UltraHDR JPEGs already known; the camera-class metadata rewrite left 22 HEICs with a wrong primary profile (19 carry the `tmap`'s PQ profile, 3 the gain map's Linear Gray; all were Display P3) and emptied gain-map/`tmap`/thumbnail profiles in 64 of 90 HEICs, image data untouched; the oldest copies (2026-06-04 snapshot, iCloud export) show the earlier GPS-removal step changed nothing else, so the whitelist rewrite made every colour/HDR change
