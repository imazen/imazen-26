Round 12 re-scored the round-11 cells against regenerated hypotheses
after review 6 found the HEIC `stored` bases carried display
transforms: bases are now decoded by heif-convert (libde265) from a
remux that drops `irot`/`imir`/`clap` (`probe --remux
--drop-transform`), each `stored` render is asserted against the
primary item's `ispe` dims, and iPhone reps whose `auxid:2` depth aux
libheif 1.17 rejects decode via a depth-dropped remux. All 18
synthetic + 21 rep HEIC hypotheses were re-rendered — the
`fixtures/hyps/` archive here differs from `2026-10-07-r11*/fixtures/hyps/`
on every HEIC row. Other changes: collapse decisions everywhere use the
fine decode-noise bound T=2 (corpus T=24 only selects the nearest
hypothesis); phase 1 is a capability probe (unsupported formats record
`n/a` with the error head, never a refusal); webkit-canvas all-zero
readbacks are `flaky`, excluded from error counts and reps
comparisons; output PNGs carrying iCCP/cICP/sRGB are converted to sRGB
(relative colorimetric) before scoring, recorded as `out_profile`;
`platform.txt` records `container_adapter=` markers and `--check`
refuses `image_id=host` on docker/browser cells; calibration.tsv names
the contributing decoders (platform columns folded in via
`CM_CALIBRATE_EXTRA`); metadata.tsv was rebuilt with the committed
warnings filter; census zen-reader stamps are thumbnail hashes named
`thumb_sha` (stamped after the census — see doc).
