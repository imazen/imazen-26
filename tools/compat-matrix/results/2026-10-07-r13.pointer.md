Round 13 adds output-tag-aware scoring and new AVIF signalling fixtures:
every `ok` cell is scored twice — raw (`verdict`, the emitted pixels
as-is) and tagged (`verdict_tagged`, output PNG's iCCP/cICP/sRGB/
gAMA+cHRM declaration interpreted to sRGB first; the declared tag lands
in `out_profile`). Precedence follows PNG-3 §11.3.2 incl. a gAMA+cHRM
converter (`gama-chrm:<name>`); a tag describing the source space when
the raw pixels already match a named reading reports
`mis-tagged:<raw verdict>`; unconvertible HDR-declared output reports
`hdr-output` (correct=n/a). `orientation_ok` grades orientation
independently of the colour verdict. Five new AVIF fixtures
(`avif-cicp-srgb-mc1`, `avif-420-srgb-mc6`, `avif-420-srgb-mc1`,
`avif-10bit-cicp-srgb`, `avif-cicp-srgb-limited`) signal
matrix/subsampling/bit-depth/range at both nclx and seq-header levels —
the seq-header matrix is patched bit-exact post-encode so the levels
agree — with `mc-709-decode`/`mc-601-decode` reference hypotheses
decoded outside the reader under test. Result: every decoding reader
honours the signalled matrix + range (all 105 LTS ok cells land the
correct collapse pair). The two Exif AVIFs are repacked to a single
iloc extent so ffmpeg's parser can read them (latest ffmpeg 9.0.1 now
`ok` on both). `png-gama-chrm-p3` separates `gama-chrm-p3` (correct)
from `p3-as-displayp3` (named near-miss, p99 3).

Provenance hardened further: phase-1 capability probes write
`probe/<adapter>.json` sidecars, never cells (a probe failure cannot
overwrite a phase-2 checkpoint); `image_id=host` cells are admitted only
for adapters positively listed via `host_adapter=` in platform.txt's
last block; `n/a` cells carry the declared-unsupported reason and are
refreshed when the fixture sha changes; `ok` cells stamp `output_sha`
at decode time (`thumb_sha` once thumbed); `--check` re-derives
matrix/versions/separations/summary.tsv byte-for-byte plus calibration
consistency against the fixtures-dir `calibration.tsv` (authoritative —
written beside hypotheses.tsv). `summary.tsv` is emitted by the tool
itself; `scripts/diff_runs.py` produces the LTS/latest move table
mechanically and excludes `flaky` cells.
